This work presents a direct digital synthesis waveform generator, described in VHDL and implemented on an FPGA, controlled by a computer through Virtual JTAG and a graphical interface in Python. The generator produces sine, ramp, sinc and arbitrary waveforms, with fine frequency tuning. An analog board, with a digital-to-analog converter and a reconstruction filter, converts the samples into voltage. The design was verified by simulation and on the bench.
