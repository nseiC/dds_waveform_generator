This work presents a direct digital synthesis waveform generator on an FPGA, described in VHDL as encapsulated blocks verified by self-checking testbenches. The frequency in hertz is converted into the tuning word by fixed-point multiplication, and control uses Virtual JTAG over the programming cable, with a Python interface. An analog board converts sine, ramp, sinc and a rewritable arbitrary waveform into voltage; the frequency measured on the bench matched the calculated one.
