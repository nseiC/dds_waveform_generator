This work presents a direct digital synthesis waveform generator, implemented in VHDL on the FPGA of the DE2-115 board and controlled by a computer through Virtual JTAG, with a graphical interface in Python. The generator produces sine, ramp, sinc and arbitrary waveforms from 0 to 262~kHz, with a resolution of 2.33~mHz. An analog board with the DAC0800 and a reconstruction filter converts the samples into voltage. The design was verified by simulation and on the bench.
